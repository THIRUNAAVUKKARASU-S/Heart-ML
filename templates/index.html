Why Heart Disease Prediction Matters

Cardiovascular diseases (CVDs) are the leading cause of death globally, taking an estimated 17.9 million lives each year. Early identification of high-risk patients is critical to implement preventative therapies and reduce cardiovascular mortality.

Traditional risk assessment scores rely on static formulas. Machine Learning methods excel at finding intricate, non-linear relationships among multiple medical attributes (such as cholesterol level, blood pressure, age, and pain indicators), offering personalized risk evaluations.

This web application demonstrates the utility of machine learning models in automated disease screening. By integrating hyperparameter optimization algorithms—such as **Jellyfish Optimization (JFO)**—the system continuously tunes model architectures to achieve optimal clinical accuracy, empowering healthcare providers with reliable diagnostic indicators.
